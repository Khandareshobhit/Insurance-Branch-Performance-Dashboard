# Insurance-Branch-Performance-Dashboard
Interactive Insurance Branch Performance Dashboard built to analyze sales targets, revenue, invoice sales, achievement, opportunities, meetings, and account executive performance using data-driven visualizations.

## Project Problem Statement

The organization does not have a single dashboard to easily track and compare branch performance across **New, Cross-Sell, and Renewal** business.

The main challenges are:

* Data is stored in different **Excel sheets and CRM systems**.
* Difficult to compare **individual targets with actual achievements**.
* Limited tracking of **meetings, opportunities, and win ratios**.
* Difficult to monitor important **branch and individual KPIs**.
* Difficult to identify **top performers and areas that need improvement**.
* Reporting takes more time and can lead to **missed opportunities**.

The project aims to bring the data together using **Power BI, Tableau, and Excel** to make performance tracking easier, improve decision-making, and help branches achieve their targets.

## Business Requirements

* Track **branch-wise target and revenue performance**.
* Monitor **New, Cross-Sell, and Renewal** business.
* Track **invoices, meetings, and opportunities**.
* Measure **achievement**.
* Compare **account executive performance**.
* Identify **top performers and key opportunities**.

## Project Objectives

* Develop an **interactive dashboard** to provide a centralized view of branch and sales performance.
* Enable **performance comparison** across branches and Account Executives.
* Analyze the **sales pipeline** using opportunity stages and expected revenue.
* Identify **business trends and performance gaps** to support better decision-making.
* Provide **clear and actionable insights** through KPIs, charts, and interactive filters.

## Process

* **Data Preparation & Modeling:** Collected, cleaned, and structured branch, sales, opportunity, invoice, meeting, and target data.
* **Data Analysis:** Analyzed targets, revenue, achievement, business type, opportunities, and account executive performance.
* **KPI & Visualization:** Created KPIs and charts to track sales performance, opportunities, invoices, meetings, and conversion.
* **Dashboard Development:** Built interactive dashboards using **Excel, Power BI, and Tableau**.
* **Insights:** Identified performance gaps, top performers, and key sales opportunities.

## Observations

* **65% placed achievement** with ₹3.39 Cr placed revenue against the target.
* **21% invoiced achievement** with ₹1.11 Cr invoiced revenue against the target.
* **Renewal** exceeded its target, while **New Business** shows a significant performance gap.
* **49 total opportunities** and **44 open opportunities** are currently tracked.
* **Renewal contributes the highest revenue share at 55%**.

## Suggestions

* Focus on improving **New Business performance** to reduce the target gap.
* Use the strong **Renewal performance** as a benchmark for other business areas.
* Closely monitor **open opportunities** to improve conversion and revenue.
* Review **account executive performance** regularly to identify areas for improvement.
* Use the dashboard for **regular performance tracking and decision-making**.
